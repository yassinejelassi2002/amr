Mechanical connectors:

Use a **bolted flange** for permanent or rarely-changed mounts such as the base-to-arm joint, since it offers maximum rigidity and handles high torque and moment loads but is not quick-swap.

Use a **kinematic mount** with dowel pins and a clamp or collar wherever precise, repeatable alignment is needed after re-docking, such as arm-to-base connections where joint calibration must stay valid. Use a dovetail with a locking pin for moderate-frequency swaps with decent lateral rigidity, suited to lighter modules like sensors or small tools.

Use a **bayonet** or **twist-lock connector** for fast, tool-less swaps where torsional load is low, such as sensor heads or light end-effectors.

Use a **quick-change ball-lock** or **pneumatic coupler** for high-frequency tool-changing at the wrist, such as swapping grippers mid-task, since these are fast and robust and commonly used in industrial tool-changers.

Use a **magnetic dock** only for very light modules with low load requiring extremely fast attachment without a latch, since it is weak against torque and unsuitable for a full arm.
