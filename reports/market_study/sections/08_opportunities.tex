\section{Where \ProjectName{} Fits In}

The modular concept -- one base, interchangeable add-ons -- fills a clear gap: no competitor offers an affordable robot repurposable across delivery, inspection, and patrol by swapping modules.

However, modularity already exists at the high end (MiR). The defensible space is \textbf{low-cost modularity for SMEs and legacy facilities, combined with local support} -- deploy on unmodified premises, price for local cost-to-labor ratio, and service in French/Arabic. Shelf-access is the most contested and capital-intensive cell -- defer it.
