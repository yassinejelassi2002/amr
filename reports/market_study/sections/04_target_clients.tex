\chapter{Target Clients}

\section{Segment Overview}

Priority follows Tunisian unit economics, not global market size. Top: universities and export-oriented industrial inspection -- where ROI compares to something other than wages.

\section{Named First Customers}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Universities}: ESPRIT, INSAT, Tunisia Polytechnic (robotics/AI programs).
    \item \textbf{Industrial inspection}: export-oriented factories in Sousse, Sfax, Ben Arous; process plants (GCT, cement, food).
    \item \textbf{Manufacturing (mid-term)}: automotive Tier-1/2 suppliers (TAA corridor).
    \item \textbf{Healthcare (secondary)}: private clinics in Tunis/Sousse (pilot).
    \item \textbf{Hospitality (showcase)}: international hotel chains already in Tunisia.
    \item \textbf{3PL/retail}: Carrefour Tunisie, Monoprix, Jumia (via RaaS).
    \item \textbf{Public institutions}: ministries/facilities (following Enova precedent).
\end{itemize}

The most valuable early win is a credible reference in inspection or a university -- fast buyers, strong demonstration effect.
