\chapter{Use Cases}

\section{Industrial Inspection (Sensor Configuration)}

Spot robots carry camera, thermal, LiDAR on fixed inspection routes \cite{standardbots_amr}. No facility changes needed. ROI based on avoided incident cost. For \ProjectName{}: sensor module on base -- lowest-friction entry.

\section{Healthcare and Internal Delivery (Compartment Configuration)}

MiR and Aethon TUG used for medication/sample transport in hospitals, reducing staff time \cite{mir_healthcare,researchgate_hospital}. \ProjectName{}: lockable compartment module for secure, auditable delivery -- relevant for hospitals and campuses.

\section{Manufacturing Intralogistics (Arm + Base)}

MiR and Robotnik RB-KAIROS+ used for line-side delivery, machine tending, kitting \cite{mir250,robotnik_kairos}. \ProjectName{}: arm-on-base -- strongest mid-term fit for export-oriented automotive/electronics.

\section{Logistics and Fulfillment (Base + Compartment)}

Geek+ goods-to-person fleets deployed regionally (e.g., Dubai CommerCity) \cite{fdatabot_amr2026}. Closest regional proof. \ProjectName{}: base + compartment/sensor, sold via service model given thin local margins.

\section{Security and Cautionary Case}

Cobalt Robotics patrol robots on subscription \cite{coherent_security}; local Enova PGuard precedent. Security best as sensor add-on to an existing base, not standalone, due to low local guard wages and incumbent.

\textbf{Cautionary}: Bossa Nova shelf-scanning robots were discontinued by Walmart \cite{dudarenok_retail_china}. A single-purpose robot with weak ROI fails -- modular reuse is more defensible.
