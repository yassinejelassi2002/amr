4 Functional Requirements This section defines the physical/performance targets and the functional content of V1. Targets are engineering estimates for the first prototype and may be refined during detailed design; any change is recorded against the relevant requirement ID. Conventions. Each requirement has a unique identifier and carries a priority and a verification method. Priority: M = mandatory (must), S = strongly desired (should), C = optional (could). Verification: T = test, D = demonstration, I = inspection, A = analysis.
