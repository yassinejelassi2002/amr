3 Objectives

\section*{3.1 Primary mission of the first prototype}

The primary mission of V1 is to design, build, and validate a custom modular mobile base for warehouse environments that can move reliably under manual control and basic au- tonomous navigation while carrying a representative payload, detecting and stopping for obstacles, and exposing a modular mounting interface for future attachments. V1 is a platform and technology demonstrator. It is explicitly not required to perform a finished commercial logistics task (such as picking or palletising); those capabilities are delivered later as modules. Success for V1 is the proven mobile base, its safety behaviour, its endurance, and its readiness to host future modules.

\section*{3.2 Project objectives}

1. Produce a self-contained mobile base (mechanics, drivetrain, power, electronics, compute) sized for warehouse aisles. 2. Demonstrate both manual (teleoperated) control and basic autonomous navigation along a defined route. 3. Demonstrate basic obstacle detection that reliably slows or stops the robot before contact. 4. Carry a representative test payload on a dedicated payload platform without loss of stability or mobility. 5.
