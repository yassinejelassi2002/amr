8 Version 1 Scope

\section*{8.1 In scope for Version 1}

1. A custom mobile base purpose-built for warehouse environments. 2. Manual (teleoperated) control and/or basic autonomous navigation. 3. Basic obstacle detection with automatic slow/stop behaviour. 4. A payload platform for carrying representative test loads. 5. Integration of the navigation sensor suite. 6. Battery and electronics integration with on-board compute. 7. A modular mounting area (mechanical plus power/data) for future additions. 8. The supporting engineering baseline: CAD, bill of materials, wiring, and test records.

\section*{8.2 Out of scope for Version 1 (future versions)}

1. Load manipulation: robotic arms, grippers, lifts, or conveyors (future modules). 2. Full autonomous fleet coordination and warehouse-management-system integration. 3. Higher payload classes beyond 50 kg. 4. Full third-party safety certification and unsupervised operation among the public workforce. 5. Multi-floor operation, outdoor operation, or operation on uneven/unsealed surfaces. 6. Advanced autonomy such as dynamic re-routing in highly crowded, fast-changing areas.
